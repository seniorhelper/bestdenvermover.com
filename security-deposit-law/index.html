Your Colorado security deposit and the one-month clock

A deposit is your money held in trust. Colorado's statute sets a deadline, a paper requirement and a penalty. Here they are as the state legislature's own staff summary describes them.

Check the current law. The only official summary we could read in full was prepared by the legislature's legal staff and last reviewed in 2018. Colorado has amended landlord-tenant statutes since, and one source we saw refers to a 2025 bill effective in 2026 that we could not verify. Read the current text of C.R.S. 38-12-103 on the legislature's site or ask a lawyer or tenant-rights group before you act. This is information, not legal advice.

The deadline

The summary says the landlord must return the full deposit within one month after the lease ends or you surrender the key to the property, whichever is later. A lease can set a longer period, but not past 60 days.

What a landlord can keep

Allowed (per the summary)Not allowed
Unpaid rent; abandonment of the premises; unpaid utility charges; unpaid repair or cleaning work you contracted forNormal wear and tear

The written statement

If any part is kept, the landlord has to send a written statement listing the exact reasons, together with a payment of the remaining deposit. The summary adds that a landlord who leaves out the statement forfeits all right to withhold any portion.

If it goes wrong

  • A landlord who willfully and wrongfully keeps a deposit can owe three times the amount wrongfully withheld, plus court costs and reasonable attorney fees.
  • The renter must notify the landlord of the intent to file suit at least seven days beforehand. A landlord who returns the whole deposit within that week avoids the triple damages, unless it missed the original deadline without an accounting.
  • In court, the landlord carries the burden of proving the withholding was not wrongful.

Other points

  • You cannot waive these rights in a lease; a clause that tries is not enforceable by the landlord.
  • If the property is sold or ownership changes, the deposit must be handed to the new owner or returned to you after lawful deductions.

What this means for your move date

Write down the day you return the key and keep proof of it, like a text, email or signed form, because the clock may start from the later of lease end and key return. Give your new mailing address in writing. If the month passes with nothing in the mail, send a dated written request for the deposit and itemization, then look at the options above.

Denver also has its own rental-licensing rules for landlords. We did not verify them and do not summarize them here.
