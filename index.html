Quick answers

How long does a Colorado landlord have to return my security deposit?

Under C.R.S. 38-12-103 as summarized by the Colorado Office of Legislative Legal Services, the landlord must return the full deposit within one month after the lease ends or you hand back the key, whichever is later, unless the lease sets a longer period of up to 60 days. Check the current statute, because Colorado has amended landlord-tenant law since that summary was reviewed.

Can a landlord keep my deposit for normal wear and tear?

No. The legislative summary says normal wear and tear cannot be withheld. A landlord may keep money for unpaid rent, abandonment, unpaid utilities and unpaid repair or cleaning that you contracted for, and must give a written list of the exact reasons.

What if my landlord doesn't send the written statement?

Per the same summary, a landlord who fails to include the written statement forfeits the right to withhold any part of the deposit.
