Privacy, in plain English

Last updated 11 November 2026. We are Eye To Ad Media, a small Denver, Colorado business. This page tells you what happens to the information you give us when you ask for small-move quotes.

Info you hand us

Name and phone are required. Email, building type, size and date are up to you. We also record the wording of the consent you agreed to, when you agreed, and which page you used, so we can prove you opted in.

Info that arrives on its own

Like any website, our server may log your IP address and browser details. There are no ad networks, analytics scripts, social buttons or embedded videos on these pages.

Where it goes

  1. To us, for a human check on every request.
  2. To at most three moving companies that clear our screen: an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit, insurance on file and a BBB A+ rating, which we confirm ourselves.

Those movers are separate businesses. They may call or text you about this move, using automated tools since you agreed to that. After that, their own privacy rules apply to what they hold.

Keeping and deleting

We hold request records only as long as they serve a purpose, such as following up or documenting consent. Ask us to erase yours at zach@eyetoad.com. Texts stop when you reply STOP.

Rights and contact

You can ask what we have, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion, and we will also notify the movers who got your request. We cannot force a mover to erase its own files, but we will ask. This is not a service for anyone under 13. Colorado law applies. Questions go to the address above.
