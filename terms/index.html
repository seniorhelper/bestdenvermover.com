1. Who we are and are not

Eye To Ad Media, of Denver, Colorado, runs this free referral service. We do not own trucks, employ movers, quote prices, or sign moving contracts. We are a go-between that hands your request to independent movers.

2. Our screening policy

Before a mover can receive requests it must show an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit, hold insurance, and carry a BBB A+ rating, and for interstate moves it must also have USDOT and MC numbers. We look each one up ourselves. That screening is a snapshot, not a promise. Please run your own check, read the estimate, and keep copies of everything you sign.

3. Movers are independent

Whatever you agree with a mover is a deal between you two. We have no say over its price, schedule or handling of your belongings, and we do not resolve disputes or claims. For household-goods complaints in Colorado, the PUC is the regulator.
